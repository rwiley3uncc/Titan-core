Networking Study Guide

OSI review:
- Layer 1 is the physical layer.
- Layer 2 handles local framing and switching.
- Layer 3 handles logical addressing and routing.

Subnetting reminder:
- A /26 network leaves 6 host bits.
- 2^6 gives 64 total addresses per subnet.
- Usable host addresses are typically total addresses minus network and broadcast.
